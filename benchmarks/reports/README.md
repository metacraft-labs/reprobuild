# Benchmark Reports

- [Reprobuild Suite M0 Baseline](reprobuild-suite-m0-baseline.md) records the
  test-suite modernization baseline inventory and links to the generated JSON
  details. Its repeated-warm cases above 20 seconds are reviewed in
  [the slow-test review inventory](reprobuild-suite-m0-slow-reviews.json).
- [Reprobuild Suite M4 Consolidation Measurement](reprobuild-suite-m4-consolidation.md)
  measures what merging pure-unit tests into shared protocol-aware binaries
  costs and buys, on three groups, and records the correctness limit that
  arrives before the cost optimum does. Its raw measurements are in
  [the consolidation measurement set](reprobuild-suite-m4-consolidation.json).
